Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 06/12/2025


Welcome to Trucking Company

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your information.


1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide to us during your interaction


2. How We Collect Your Information

We collect information through:

  • Email communications
  • SMS communications
  • Direct conversations


3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Communicate with you about your business inquiries
  • Share real-time information such as pictures, truck locations, and coordinates
  • Provide customer service and support
  • Improve our services


4. Sharing Your Information

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your phone number or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only when necessary to deliver our services, with trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements.


5. SMS Consent

SMS opt-in or phone numbers for the purpose of SMS are not being shared with any third party or affiliate company for marketing purposes.


6. Opt-Out

You can opt-out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying “STOP” to any message.


By using our website, you consent to our Privacy Policy.
